# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook.
This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the
formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

**Required specifications:**
- Format: PNG
- Wide-landscape format
- Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
- This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

## Subject & Tone

*Scratch Programming* is a textbook that introduces visual,
block-based programming with Scratch to children ages 8–12, teaching core
computational-thinking concepts — sequencing, loops, events, broadcasting,
conditionals, and variables — through hands-on creative projects like
animations, interactive stories, and games. The intended audience is
elementary/middle-school children with no prior coding experience, plus the
parents and teachers guiding them. The visual tone should be playful,
colorful, and inviting — bright and energetic like a cartoon, echoing
Scratch's own kid-friendly visual identity, while staying clean and
uncluttered enough for the title to remain legible.

## Title

Place the title **Scratch Programming** in the center of the image, in a
clean, highly legible, slightly rounded sans-serif font (playful but not
childish/handwritten). Use a white font color with a subtle drop shadow or
dark scrim behind it so it stays readable against the busy montage
background. Keep the title short enough to render at a large size — do not
shrink it to fit; instead simplify the background directly behind the text.

## Background Montage

Arrange a montage of the following 8 concepts around the title, each
rendered in a consistent illustration style (see Style below) so the
composition reads as one image rather than a collage of unrelated styles:

1. **Sprite on the Stage** — a friendly cartoon sprite (a simple animal like a turtle or
   rocket character, distinct from the mascot) standing on the Scratch
   stage backdrop, with the stage's characteristic light-blue background.
2. **Color-Coded Blocks Palette** — a vertical strip of Scratch's
   signature snap-together puzzle-piece blocks, each category in its own
   bold flat color (blue for Motion, purple for Looks, pink for Sound,
   yellow for Events, orange for Control).
3. **X/Y Coordinate Grid** — a simple Cartesian grid centered on (0,0)
   with a small sprite icon positioned at a labeled coordinate point,
   axes drawn in a bright contrasting color.
4. **Snapped Block Script** — three or four puzzle-shaped code blocks
   snapped together vertically into a single script stack, showing how
   blocks interlock.
5. **Forever/Repeat Loop** — a C-shaped loop block wrapped around a small
   stack of action blocks, with a circular arrow icon suggesting
   repetition.
6. **Broadcast Message Burst** — two sprite icons connected by a
   dashed arc with a small radiating "broadcast" icon in between,
   suggesting sprite-to-sprite communication.
7. **Pen Extension Spirograph** — a colorful geometric spiral or
   star pattern drawn as a thin rainbow-colored line trail, evoking the
   Pen extension's drawing projects.
8. **Simple Maze Game Scene** — a small top-down maze or platformer
   scene with a player sprite, a scoreboard readout, and a goal flag,
   suggesting the games unit.

## Mascot

Place the book's mascot, **Scratch the Cat**, in the lower-left corner,
sized so it does not overlap the title text. The mascot is a warm
tangerine-orange cartoon cat (hex `#FF8C1A`) with cream/light-peach
accents on its belly, inner ears, paws, and muzzle (hex `#FFE0B2`), big
round curious eyes with dark pupils, small triangular ears, soft whisker
lines, and a warm closed-mouth smile. It has chibi-style proportions with
a slightly oversized head, no clothing or accessories, and is drawn in a
flat 2D cartoon/vector style with bold clean black outlines and bright
saturated flat colors (no gradients or textures). Use the character's
**welcome pose** — one arm raised in a friendly wave, an open, inviting
stance — matching the reference image at `docs/img/mascot/welcome.png`.
The mascot should read as an original illustration, not a reproduction of
the MIT/Scratch Team's copyrighted Scratch Cat sprite or logo.

## Style & Composition

- Illustration style: flat 2D vector cartoon illustration, bold clean
  black outlines, bright saturated flat colors, no gradients or
  textures — matching the mascot's own art style so the whole cover
  feels like one consistent world.
- Color palette: warm tangerine orange and cream (drawn from the mascot
  and the book's orange/deep-orange Material theme), accented with sky
  blue and teal for the stage, grid, and background elements.
- Lighting/mood: bright, cheerful, sunny, and optimistic — no shadows or
  moody lighting.
- Composition: title centered, montage elements arranged in a loose ring
  or grid around it, generous negative space immediately behind the
  title so it stays readable, mascot anchored in the lower-left corner
  clear of the title and montage elements.

## Avoid

- Do not render dense paragraphs of illegible text anywhere in the image;
  short labels or single characters on blocks are fine.
- Avoid generic stock-photo cliches (handshakes, isolated lightbulbs,
  people pointing at whiteboards).
- Avoid photorealistic human faces or photorealistic rendering of any
  element — everything should be flat cartoon/vector style.
- Do not let montage elements visually compete with or overlap the title
  or the mascot.
- Do not copy MIT/Scratch Team's official Scratch Cat sprite, logo, or
  wordmark verbatim — this mascot is an original character inspired by,
  but distinct from, that trademarked design.
